# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must provide a professional, harassment-free environment. Be
respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, accept correction, and
make room for different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised
attention, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and
repeated disruption of project work.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, releases, and other project spaces. It also applies when a person is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public conduct issue. The
maintainer can remove content, restrict participation, or ban a participant.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical, subject to safety and legal
requirements.

## Attribution

This policy is a concise project policy informed by the
